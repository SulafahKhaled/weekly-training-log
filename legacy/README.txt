WEEKLY TRAINING TRACKER — how to use
=====================================

1. Keep index.html and app.js in the SAME folder (don't separate them).
2. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser. That's it — no install, no server needed.
3. Requires an internet connection only for the video previews (they stream from YouTube). Everything else — timers, checkboxes, progress — works fully offline.
4. Your daily checkmarks are saved in your browser's local storage and reset automatically each new day. Use "Reset today's progress" on any day to clear it manually.
5. Tip: on your phone, open index.html in your browser then use "Add to Home Screen" for a quick launcher icon.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- All 7 days from your plan (Sun–Sat), each with tap-to-expand exercises
- Resistance days: tap each set to check it off — a rest timer starts automatically
- Isometric holds (planks, wall sit, superman, etc.): auto-cycling hold/rest timer
- HIIT days (Tue/Sat): full work/rest circuit timer that cycles through all moves across 4 rounds
- Every exercise has a tap-to-preview YouTube demo, playable right on the page
- Warm-up, Cool-down, and Mobility tabs with dedicated routines and videos (not detailed in your original PDF)
